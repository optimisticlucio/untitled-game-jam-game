# Idea List

Ideas we had, with *zero* organization, just so we don't drop them.

- The instruments will be shown initially with both a picture and text, and as the challenge increases we remove the image and only leave text.
- You can see the actual surgery in the background, and if you hand them the wrong instrument, they just *throw* it at the patient. Like stabbing someone with a surgical knife instead of tossing it.
- The selection of objects should be hold and drag rather than a click, to help with issues of misclicking and have the object be easier to peek at (you hold it and it moves to the top).
- The game is fundamentally physics based (as one of the required issues to solve is recognizing within the reflections and movement of objects in 3D), however physics based games have an issue of being chaotic and not skill-based, which this game (as an educational game) must be. As such, maybe the physics should be limited to within the tray of objects, and any carried objects should be intangible (so people don't intentionally jumble up the tray).
